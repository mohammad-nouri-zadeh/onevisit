# Voiceover script (read it yourself)

`onevisit-final.mp4` is silent (2:02). The same film is cut into six clips in `clips/`, at scene changes, so you can record one clip at a time and join them. Times are when to start each line, counted from the start of that clip (in brackets: the time in the full video). The lines were timed at a calm pace of about 150 words a minute. If you run long, slow the clip down slightly or trim a word or two. The numbers must stay as written.

## 1-hook.mp4 (19.5 s)

The first 11 seconds are silent: the AI-generated scenes, then the title.

- **0:11** (0:11): "In 2024, nineteen thousand, seven hundred and fifty-five people registered in Milan arriving from abroad."
  *The number counts up on screen, with its source, ds1959.*
