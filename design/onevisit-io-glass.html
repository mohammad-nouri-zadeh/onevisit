What makes it different from a general chatbot

Every fact has its sourceEach requirement links to the City page or dataset it came from, with the date it was checked.
It says when it doesn't knowA requirement without a verified source is shown as unknown, never guessed.
Claude works while you use itIt understands your case, asks only the questions that change the answer, builds your checklist and finds the office.
A person decidesThe desk officer makes the final check; City staff approve every correction.
