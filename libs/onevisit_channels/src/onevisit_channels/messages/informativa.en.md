**Short privacy notice**

- **You are talking to an artificial intelligence**: OneVisit uses Claude, a model by Anthropic. The information comes from the official pages it cites; the decision on your case is always made by the officer at the desk.
- **What we keep**: only structured case fields (service, chosen answers, week and office of the appointment, outcome). **What you type is never stored.** We never ask for your name, tax code, address, document photos or credentials.
- **Contact (optional)**: email or phone only if you choose to receive messages; we keep it encrypted and delete it automatically after the last planned message (exact period to be confirmed with the DPO).
- **Purposes, with separate consents**: (1) a reminder before the appointment; (2) a question about how it went, after the appointment; (3) a notice if the City corrects information that concerns you.
- **Withdrawal**: every message has a link to manage your consents or delete your contact; by SMS just reply **STOP**.
- **Data controller**: Comune di Milano (da confermare con il DPO).
