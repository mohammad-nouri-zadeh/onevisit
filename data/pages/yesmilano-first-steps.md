### BEFORE ARRIVING

Follow the instructions of your University/ Academy for enrollment, UniversItaly registration, and [Visa application](https://www.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/student-visa).

Students from the EU, ask for the European Health Insurance Card, and S1 form if needed. Every student can enjoy the public health service in Italy, for free or by paying a yearly fee (see below).

You can apply for your Tax Code / Codice Fiscale from the Italian Consulate of your area. You can also get it when in Milano (see below).

- [Read our guide to student visa](https://www.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/student-visa)

### GET YOUR TAX CODE

The Tax Code or Codice Fiscale is a unique identification code that you'll need for nearly all administrative tasks. It’s free to obtain and is valid for a lifetime.

It can be requested in person to the nearest Revenue Agency office.

- [LEARN MORE](https://www.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/get-italian-tax-code-codice-fiscale)

### GET YOUR TRANSPORTATION PASS

Milano has an extensive public transportation system, including buses, trams, and the metro (subway). Purchase tickets at metro stations, news stands, or using the ATM Milano official app.

The Municipality of Milano is the M1 zone (called area urbana):

- Single ticket: € 2.20 (M1-M3) You can also tap your bank/credit card on the totem.
- Students under 27: € 22 per month or € 200 per year​
- Regular fare: € 39 per month or € 330 per year

If you live or travel outside the Municipality of Milano, the pass must be purchased depending on the number of zones traveled (from Mi1 up to Mi9 zones).

- [LEARN MORE](https://www.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/get-your-student-transportation-pass)

### OBTAIN YOUR RESIDENCE PERMIT CARD

If you are subject to a visa for study, you are required to apply for the residence permit when in Milano. Apply within 8 working days of arrival and submit it at a Post Office that features .

### BENEFIT OF THE ITALIAN HEALTHCARE SYSTEM

From General Practitioner visits to emergency care, the National healthcare system has you covered.

If you are an EU exchange student, you can use your EHIC (European health insurance card) while in Milano.

Full-course or non-EU students have the option to obtain insurance at a significantly affordable rate by enrolling into the Italian healthcare system.

- [LEARN HOW](https://www.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/national-health-service-students-step-step)

### REGISTER AS A RESIDENT IN MILANO

Temporary registration is available for EU students staying temporarily in Milano more than 4 months and up to one year, e.g. exchange students.

Just fill out the forms and send an e-mail with the required attachments.

Residence registration is the best option for those planning an extended stay in Milano. EU students resident in Milano can opt to vote from here for EU elections. Non-EU students can apply for residence right after collecting their residence permit card. Italian citizens can immediately transfer their residence in town as well.

- [REGISTER AS TEMPORARY RESIDENT EU CITIZEN](/en/study/how-to/get-temporary-residence-milano)
