The Tax Code / Fiscal code / Codice Fiscale is a code made of letters and numbers that identifies a person in public administration forms, in private contracts, and money transfers. It looks like this: MLNYSE17A41F205U

It is free to obtain, and it is valid for a lifetime.

Fun Fact: a tax code is calculated on your surname (three letters), name (three letters), year, month, day and sex (five characters), place of birth, and a checksum letter.

### FROM ABROAD

It is no longer possible to request the Italian Tax Code (Codice Fiscale) from a Consular service office at an Italian Embassy or Consulate. It can only be requested upon arrival in Italy.

Updated 15/05/2025

### FROM MILANO

#### Get the Tax Code from the Revenue Agency

For all incoming international students who are already physically in Italy, the  tax code can be requested to the Revenue Agency/Agenzia delle Entrate

For Incoming International Students in the Milano area, the application will be done in person/call/videocall according to the following procedure:

1. Book your appointment at a local office go on the [Revenue Agency Website](https://prenotazioneweb.agenziaentrate.gov.it/PrenotazioneWeb/prenotazione.action?gruppoServizi=IDEN) and select Per te stesso (Book for yourself).
2. Mark Prima richiesta di attribuzione del codice fiscale (Request your tax code for the first time)
3. Fill out the form with your surname / cognome , name / nome and e-mail.
4. On the next step write Milano and proceed: you will see a list of the Revenue agency's local offices (Uffici territoriali) and the first available appointments for each
5. You can see where the 6 offices (Ufficio Territoriale Agenzia delle Entrate) in town are located [on Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/d/edit?mid=1vNO3lbyYfTL8rVezdw8zqeHPLKnHlt8&usp=sharing). All are conveniently accessible via public transportation.
6. Choose one and click on In presenza/Chiamata/Videochiamata and select the appointment slot more convenient to you.
7. As reason for your appointment / Motivo di richiesta appuntamento\*: write: " YesMilano CF Studente internazionale ". Proceed by clicking Avanti
8. Solve the captcha and click Avanti
9. You will see the booking confirmation and receive a copy of it by e-mail.

   Before your appointment, we recommend downloading [the AA4/8 pdf application form](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/278995/Form+AA48+Editable_AA4_8_Modello_ING.pdf/e32d806e-92e3-05a5-37ac-ab270ff86a74) and filling it out following the instructions (please see next paragraph).

   Print it out and sign it by pen (electronic signatures are not accepted).

   On the day of the appointment, remember to bring:
   -your application form
   -your ID or Passport and your visa and permit of stay/receipt (if applicable)
   -your certificate of enrollment in the University/Academy/School

DISCLAIMER: We strongly recommend booking an appointment and going in person with your completed application form and all required documents. This is the smoothest way to obtain your tax code, ensuring you receive the document in just 15 minutes.

- [Book your appointment](https://prenotazioneweb.agenziaentrate.gov.it/PrenotazioneWeb/prenotazione.action?gruppoServizi=IDEN)

### How to fill out the application form

Download the [AA4/8 pdf application form](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/278995/Form+AA48+Editable_AA4_8_Modello_ING.pdf/e32d806e-92e3-05a5-37ac-ab270ff86a74) and open it with a pdf reader (such as the free Adobe Acrobat DC) that allows you to fill it in. Alternatively, you can print it out and fill it in clear capital letters.

PART A

- Check the box “direct application for yourself” if you are applying for yourself.
- In the “Applicant Type Code” section, write the number that identifies you (01 for students).
- Check the box “Allocation of a Tax code” if you are requesting the Tax code for the first time.
- Check the box “Request for a Tax code” if you want to receive the Tax code card at your home address.

PART B

- Fill it out with your personal details (as they appear in your ID/Passport).
- Date format dd/mm/yyyy.

PART C

- Place your address in Milano -if you already know it- which will act as your domicile for fiscal communications. If you do not have an address in Milano yet, leave it blank. N.B. You do not need to write anything in Area/Other.

PART D

- Fill it out with your address abroad.

SIGNATURES

- Write the date of the day you are filling the application form out (dd/mm/yyyy).
- Sign by pen. ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES ARE NOT ACCEPTED.

### How to check if your Tax Code is valid

#### A useful tool for checking the validity of Codice Fiscale

Verifica Codice Fiscale tool

/

Although the tax code can be generated in a "formally correct" way by using unofficial online tools, only Tax codes issued by the Italian Revenue Agency/ Agenzia delle Entrate are valid by law.

A tool is available on the [Revenue Agency website](https://telematici.agenziaentrate.gov.it/VerificaCF/Scegli.do?parameter=verificaCf) to check its validity.

Type your tax code in the Codice Fiscale field, solve the captcha and press the Invia button.

If the output is CODICE FISCALE VALIDO, your tax code is official and valid.

If the output is CODICE FISCALE NON VALIDO (and you did not misspell it), you need to apply for the valid one.

- [Check if your tax code is valid](https://telematici.agenziaentrate.gov.it/VerificaCF/Scegli.do?parameter=verificaCf)

### Tax code is not mandatory for the registration of a Rent contract

A message from the Agenzia della Entrate (Italian Revenue Agency):
Numerous students have encountered obstacles in obtaining the Tax Code (Codice Fiscale) prior to signing a lease agreement. However, it's crucial to note that the Agenzia delle Entrate has clarified that the Tax Code is not a prerequisite for contract registration. This official stance can be effectively communicated to your landlord by presenting the corresponding [official statement.](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/4988606/Risoluzione+registrazione+atti+soggetti+esteri+privi+di+CF+pub.pdf/6995eec2-66a1-0b6c-0361-296ed2d0a3a1)

[Here](https://www.fiscooggi.it/rubrica/normativa-e-prassi/articolo/contratti-daffitto-non-residenti-registrabili-senza-codice)'s another reference from the professional press.

### F.A.Q.

1. What to do in case of incorrect data on the tax identification number card?

In case of incorrect data shown on the tax identification number card, a replacement must be required to any office of the Italian Revenue Agency, showing a valid identification document.

2. What to do in case of loss or theft?

You can request a duplicate of the tax identification number by filling the [request online](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/schede/istanze/richiesta+ts_cf/richiesta+duplicato+tessera+sanitaria+e+codice+fiscale). As an alternative, you can request it directly to a territorial office of the Italian Revenue Agency.

3. What in case of a minor?

If a minor needs a tax code in Italy, the request must be submitted by a parent or legal guardian. Both the minor and the parent must go together to the Agenzia delle Entrate office (appointment required) with the minor’s passport, the university acceptance or enrollment letter, the parent’s identity document, and the completed AA4/8 form (Part A, Section I, option “T – Application for a third party”). For non-EU parents, proof of parental relationship is required, such as the child’s name on the passport or a translated and apostilled birth certificate. The application in person is usually recommended.
